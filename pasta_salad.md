# Pasta Salad

## Ingredients

- High Protein Pasta
- Costco Chicken
- Olive Garden Italian Dressing
- 1 Cucumber
- Feta Cheese
- 1 Bell Pepper
- Cherry Tomatoes
- Pinto Beans

## Instructions

- Salt water and bring to boil
- Cook pasta until fork tender
- Drain and leave pasta to cool in fridge
- Mince vegetables
- Debone Chicken
- Mix together in large bowl
- Add pasta (when cool enough)
- Top with crumbled feta and dressing to taste
